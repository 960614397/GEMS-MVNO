Section 01

What is an MVNO?

A Mobile Virtual Network Operator (MVNO) is a brand that sells mobile services without owning the underlying radio network. It rides on a host MNO's coverage and competes on brand, customer experience, and the value layered on top of the SIM. For GEMS, that "value layered on top" is healthcare, wellness, family protection and structural member loyalty.

The Three-Party Model

PartyRole
MNO — Mobile Network OperatorOwns the towers, spectrum and core network. Provides wholesale capacity. (MTN — pan-African coverage)
MVNE — Mobile Virtual Network Enabler (That's us — DSG)Operates the BSS, OCS, provisioning and day-to-day commercial running of the MVNO.
MVNO — Mobile Virtual Network Operator (GEMS)Owns the brand, the member relationship and the go-to-market. The face of the business.

Why Restricted Schemes Launch MVNOs

01

Non-comparative member proposition

Bundle data, voice and wellness rewards into the GEMS benefit promise — at a price no MNO retail offer can apples-to-apples compare with.

02

Greater member entanglement

Mobile connects to every day of a member's life — claims, scripts, screenings, fitness. Loyalty becomes structural, not annual-renewal-dependent.

03

First-party behavioural & wellness data

Passively collected usage data (POPIA-compliant, opt-in) feeds personalisation, chronic adherence and risk-based pricing models.

04

New high-margin revenue streams

Layered VAS — telehealth, fintech, security, family insurance — unlock margin and revenue that pure scheme contributions cannot.

40%+ — Average MVNO churn without strong CX. The MVNOs that win don't compete on price — they win on relevance. For GEMS, relevance is health, family and rand-stretch. — MVNO Nation CX Study

Section 03

How We Build MVNOs

Our operating model abstracts the technical and operational complexity of running an MVNO so GEMS can focus on members, propositions and growth. Five deliverables, sequenced — from commercial viability to launch.

The Three-Layer Operating Model

Each party plays a role they are best placed to play. The MVNE sits in the middle and makes the model work.

Host MNO — MTN
Network & Infrastructure

Provides network infrastructure, wholesale billing, SIM procurement, voucher distribution. Pan-African coverage for cross-border members.

MVNE — DSG
Operations & Platform

Manages technical operations, member management, billing & invoicing, inbound member care, business support, integrations to claims & scheme systems.

MVNO — GEMS
Brand & Member Experience

Owns brand, member marketing, sales, distribution, scheme integration and wellness loyalty.

Five Deliverables — Sequenced

Every engagement starts with commercial viability and ends with an MVP ready to take to GEMS members.

01 — BM

Business Model

Granular 60-month business plan and commercial viability analysis. Acquisition, usage, revenue, cost-to-serve, bad debt, peak funding requirement.

02 — OM

Operating Model

High-level technical architecture, relational diagram between parties and draft service levels covering network, platform, billing and CX.

03 — GTMP

Go-to-Market Plan

Sales, distribution, member care and full lifecycle management — from acquisition through retention to win-back.

04 — ICX

Integrated CX

Member journeys and high-level business processes designed for the channels GEMS members actually use — WhatsApp, USSD, app, walk-in.

05 — MVP

Min. Viable Proposition

Packages, deals and specific products & services ready to go to market at launch — Member, Family, Rural, Wellness and Provider Connect.

VAS: Where MVNOs Actually Win

Connectivity gets you to parity. Value-Added Services get you to margin, stickiness and lifetime member value. For GEMS, the right VAS stack is the one that already lives in a member's life.

VAS delivers three outcomes from one strategic lever:

Revenue
New high-margin income streams
Differentiation
No restricted scheme can be replicated
Retention
Relevant services = lower voluntary lapse

Platform Revenue Driver

The MVNO becomes the primary driver of GEMS member platform adoption — turning every claim submission, benefit check and provider interaction into an engagement and value moment. Zero-rated portal data, rewards per qualifying milestone, in-app benefit prompts and real-time claims status.

Higher member platform adoption · Higher benefit utilisation · Frictionless healthcare access — always rewarded
The portal stops being an admin tool and becomes a value-generating health ecosystem. MTN connectivity ensures scheme access is never out of reach — even in underserved communities.

Use Case 3: Preventive Health & Wellness Engagement
Daily Engagement Driver

Enable high-frequency, real-time wellness participation at scale. Activity rewards, chronic check-in incentives, medication adherence alerts, micro-rewards for every preventive milestone. Zero-rated data for all wellness platform and health tracking activity.

Increased daily wellness engagement · Habitual platform usage · GEMS becomes the default everyday health solution
Most medical schemes pay for illness. GEMS rewards you for staying well. Every wellness interaction strengthens the member relationship.

Use Case 4: Low-Income & Entry-Level Members
Inclusion Growth Engine

Enable lower-income public servants on entry-level benefit options to use GEMS's connected platform as their primary health management tool. Affordable SIM plans bundled with entry-option benefits, instant benefit confirmation, zero-rated provider tools, preventive care incentives.

GEMS becomes the real-time health partner for lower-income public servants · Deeper penetration of remote & entry-level segment · Measurable health outcomes from an underserved base
No competitor bundles public-sector health cover, digital scheme management and connectivity as a single, reward-driven proposition.

Use Case 5: Government & Public Sector Ecosystem Integration
Strategic Differentiator

Leverage the broader government employer ecosystem — PERSAL, DPSA, National Treasury, public sector unions — as a structural commercial advantage. Payroll deduction, HR portal integration, government onboarding flow connectivity, co-branded provider/pharmacy/wellness offers across the employee lifecycle.

Higher cross-sell & lifetime member value · Institutional backing & co-investment · A structural moat no other open scheme can replicate
This is GEMS's single biggest competitive advantage. No other medical scheme has the employer density, payroll integration and public sector mandate to bundle connectivity at this scale.

Use Case 6: Rural & Remote Member Connectivity
Underserved Community Value

Extend GEMS's strength across all nine provinces into a seamless connected experience for rural and underserved members — powered by MTN's pan-South African network. Affordable rural bundles with zero-rated scheme access, provider locator, telemedicine, real-time referral & pre-auth alerts, community health worker connectivity.

Seamless healthcare access for remote members · Higher telemedicine & digital health adoption · Stronger loyalty where connectivity matters most
GEMS already serves public servants in every corner of South Africa. The MVNO makes that commitment real — connected, accessible and rewarded, from Johannesburg to the most remote rural district.

Higher household retention & lower lapse · Recurring value from active dependants · Stronger emotional loyalty compounding across the family unit

Higher provider digital authorisation adoption · Higher claims processing efficiency · Reduced member out-of-pocket exposure

The MVNO becomes the core engagement and rewards layer across the entire GEMS member base — driving retention through habitual, rewarded health behaviour. Tiered rewards — GEMS Sapphire / Emerald / Diamond — monthly data & airtime incentives, behaviour-linked benefits, gamified app mechanics.

Reduced member lapse · Increased lifetime scheme value · Higher engagement frequency across all touchpoints
Airtime and data are the most immediately valuable rewards in this market. GEMS becomes the scheme that keeps members connected — literally.

Use Case 10: Data-Driven Member Health Intelligence Platform
Future State / Vision

The MVNO becomes the data backbone of the entire GEMS ecosystem. Member health analytics combining claims, mobile and wellness data; personalised preventive care nudges; predictive lapse modelling; anonymised population-health insights for DPSA, National Treasury and public health partners.

GEMS evolves from medical scheme administrator into a data-driven population health and member intelligence platform for South African public sector healthcare
Most schemes know how many claims they paid. GEMS will know who submitted them, what conditions are emerging, what benefits go unused, and when members are likely to lapse. That intelligence is worth more than any single premium margin.
